FIELDMEDIC OFFLINE REFERENCE
START ADULT MASS-CASUALTY TRIAGE

PURPOSE
This reference summarizes the START (Simple Triage and Rapid Treatment)
adult mass-casualty triage framework for the FieldMedic demonstration.

This is decision-support reference material only.
FieldMedic does not replace trained medical judgment, EMS protocols,
incident command, or emergency medical services.

TRIAGE TAGS

RED - IMMEDIATE
Patient has severe or life-threatening injuries requiring immediate
treatment and transportation.

YELLOW - DELAYED
Patient has serious injuries, but immediate treatment or transportation
can be delayed based on the triage assessment.

GREEN - MINOR
Patient has relatively minor injuries and is unlikely to deteriorate
rapidly. Ambulatory patients may fall into this category during START
triage.

BLACK - EXPECTANT
Patient is deceased or unlikely to survive given injury severity,
available resources, or both.

START ASSESSMENT FACTORS

START considers:

1. Ability to walk
2. Presence or absence of spontaneous breathing
3. Respiratory rate
4. Perfusion, such as radial pulse or capillary refill
5. Mental status, including ability to obey commands

A respiratory rate greater than 30 breaths per minute is one finding
used by the START adult algorithm when determining triage category.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

Triage is dynamic. A patient's condition may change and reassessment
may be necessary.

START is intended for mass-casualty triage. It is not a complete
individual diagnostic system.

FieldMedic must not claim that its output is a confirmed diagnosis.

FieldMedic must not fabricate information that was not entered by
the responder.

When FieldMedic identifies insufficient information, it should say so
rather than infer missing clinical findings.
